# Expanded StarTap content

The current global-v4 edition contains 65 stars: the original ten story questions plus the 55 additions listed below. Five questions are selected per UTC day, without repeating a star during the thirteen-day cycle. The collection then repeats; this is labelled in the game.

Older links explicitly using origins-v1, stories-v2, or global-v3 retain their original question banks and ordering. The global-v3 bank remains at 50 stars.

The wheel is a daily reveal, not a random draw. All segments start masked. During the animation only the segment under the pointer reveals its initials; the final segment remains highlighted briefly before opening the question. Skip and reduced-motion controls are supported.

Country and territory labels use Natural Earth label points and local Open Sans glyphs. Country flags are local SVGs. See public/MAP-SOURCES.txt and public/flags/SOURCES.txt.

Portraits: the existing five credited photos remain; the other stars use monogram portraits. No unlicensed photographs were added.
