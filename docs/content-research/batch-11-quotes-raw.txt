  • Naruhito (born February 23, 1960, Tokyo, Japan) is the emperor of Japan from 2019.
  • He is Japan’s 126th emperor, and, according to tradition, traces his lineage directly to Jimmu, the legendary first emperor of Japan.
  • At birth, Naruhito became heir presumptive to the Japanese imperial throne, being the eldest son of Akihito, then the crown prince, and his wife, Michiko, and grandson of the emperor Hirohito.
  • His status was elevated to that of crown prince in 1989 (formally invested on February 23, 1991), following the death of his grandfather and the ascension of his father to the throne.
  • Naruhito was raised in the imperial palace in central Tokyo and attended Gakushuin University in the city, graduating in 1982 with a bachelor’s degree in history.
  • Naruhito was the first heir to the Japanese throne to study abroad, and, in addition to pursuing his schoolwork, he was exposed to such ordinary activities as doing his own laundry and using a credit card.
  • Naruhito first met Owada Masako, his future bride, in 1986.
  • The couple’s one child, Princess Aiko, was born in 2001.
  • Naruhito had hoped that the couple would be less bound by the reclusive and traditional strictures of the Imperial Household Agency, and in their first years of marriage he and his wife were able to travel together.

##### margrethe-ii (Margrethe II)

  • Margrethe II (born April 16, 1940, Copenhagen, Denmark) was the queen of Denmark who ascended to the throne upon the death of her father, King Frederik IX, and occupied the Danish throne for exactly 52 years.
  • Born a week after the German invasion of Denmark during World War II, Margrethe spent the war years in Denmark and then attended school in Copenhagen.
  • In 1953, following a change in the Danish constitution to permit female succession to the throne, Margrethe, the king’s eldest daughter, assumed the title of “throne heiress”—i.e., crown princess, although that title in Denmark had denoted the wife of a male heir to the throne.
  • Their first child, Crown Prince Frederik, was born on May 26, 1968, and a second son, Prince Joachim, on June 7, 1969.
  • Throughout her reign she has been known for her passion for archaeology and the arts.
  • Having studied archaeology at Cambridge, she participated in excavations both in Denmark and abroad.
  • As she prepared to step down from the throne, more than 85 percent Danes polled indicated that they supported the royal family to at least some degree.
  • In her New Year’s Eve speech on December 31, 2023, she surprised observers when she announced that she would abdicate in favor of Crown Prince Frederik on the 52nd anniversary of her ascent to the throne.
